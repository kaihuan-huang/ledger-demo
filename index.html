2. The ledger

      Where the rules come from

      • From the IPOT POS: settlement is one transaction, so the charge, closing the bill, freeing the table and the cash-drawer entry land together or not at all. Refunds only go against captured payments, never beyond what is left including the tip, and record a reason and an approver. A void records who did it and why, and a voided bill can't be settled. Items keep the price the cashier saw when they were ordered. At IPOT these actions are written to an audit log.
      • From the governance-platform rebuild: the append-only, hash-chained history and a verifier that walks it; its September 2026 run verified 328 incident events.
      • Added here: anchoring. A hash chain alone only proves rows agree with each other; someone who can rewrite the table can recompute every hash (attack 3). Keeping the latest hash outside the database is what makes a rewrite visible.
