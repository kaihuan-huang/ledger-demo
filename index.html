3. Settlement: four calls, or one transaction

Before my change, the till settled a bill with four requests: capture the payment, close the bill, move the table, write the drawer. Each committed on its own. I replaced them with POST /orders/{id}/settle, which does all four inside one transaction. Pick where the process dies and run both.

Three holes the same change closed

  • Any signed-in user could close an unpaid bill. PATCH /orders/{id} with status=closed skipped every payment check, back-filled paid_total from the total and freed the table. Closing now goes through /close or /settle only.
  • An abandoned payment attempt raised the guest's bill. The tip was added to order.total when the intent was created and never removed when it was cancelled. The tip now lives on the payment that carries it; the bill's tip is the sum over captured payments.
  • A paid table stayed dirty forever. dirty was written in one place and cleared nowhere. Settle takes the target floor status, and PATCH /tables/{id}/floor-status writes it explicitly.
